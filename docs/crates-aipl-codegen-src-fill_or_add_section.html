pub fn fill_or_add_section(contents: str, section: str, body: str) -> str

Replaces the body of the --- section --- block in contents with body (dropping the old body up to the next header line or EOF), preserving everything else; or, when contents doesn't already carry that section, appends --- section ---\\n<body>\\n (after dropping trailing newlines) instead of requiring it to exist.
