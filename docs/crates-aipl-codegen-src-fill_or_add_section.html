fill_section fn 

fn fill_section(contents: str, section: str, body: str) -> str

Replaces the body of the --- section --- block in contents with body (dropping the old body up to the next header line or EOF), preserving everything else. Only ever called once fill_or_add_section has confirmed section is present, so the "not found" fallthrough below is never actually hit by a real caller — it falls out of the line-walk for free, not worth special-casing away.

contains_section fn 

fn contains_section(contents: str, section: str) -> bool

Whether contents already carries a --- section --- header. lines mirrors plain split("\n") (a spurious trailing empty element for a final \n), unlike Rust's str::lines() — harmless here since an empty line never parses as a header.

fill_or_add_section fn pub 

pub fn fill_or_add_section(contents: str, section: str, body: str) -> str

Replaces the body of the --- section --- block in contents with body (dropping the old body up to the next header line or EOF), preserving everything else; or, when contents doesn't already carry that section, appends --- section ---\\n<body>\\n (after dropping trailing newlines) instead of requiring it to exist.
