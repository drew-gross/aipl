pub fn tuple_windows<T: any>(self: T[]) -> (T, T)[]

Every adjacent pair of elements, overlapping: [1, 2, 3].tuple_windows() is [(1, 2), (2, 3)]. Fewer than two elements give no pairs at all. Generic over the element type and callable as a method; a str receiver pairs its chars.
