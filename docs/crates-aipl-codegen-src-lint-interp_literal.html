The interp_literal lint: `a: {'a'}` / `aa: {"aa"}` — a string or char literal as the whole of a template interpolation. The interpolation renders the literal as its text, which is exactly what the surrounding template would have said had the text been written in place: `a: a`. The braces, the quotes and the render are all spent on nothing.

Only a literal standing alone is this shape — {"a" +++ s} computes something — and only the escaping forms of it: a """ literal is raw, and its contents may need escapes a template would decode (\{, a backtick), so it is a way of getting verbatim text into a template rather than a detour.

The hit points at the literal.
