pub fn common_space_prefix(a: str, b: str) -> i64

Returns the length of the longest common prefix of a and b made up entirely of space characters — their shared space-only indentation. Stops at the first index where the strings differ or the (matching) byte isn't a space (a tab or letter ends the count).
