pub fn intersection<T: any>(self: #{T}, other: #{T}) -> #{T}

The elements both self and other hold, as a set. Either operand empty, or the two disjoint, gives the empty set.

Callable as a method (wanted.intersection(found)). Both operands are sets of any order; the result is always unordered, for the reason difference gives — and difference is the one to reach for when you want the elements other does not hold.
