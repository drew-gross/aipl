companion_files fn 

pub fn companion_files(src: str) -> Companion[]!str

The --- file: <path> --- sections of src, in order, as path/contents pairs. Everything outside such a section — the program text and the other section kinds — is ignored, and a section body keeps its inner blank lines but drops trailing newlines.

A file: section whose path is empty, or contains a backslash, is an err: both would resolve unpredictably when staged, and staging a case somewhere unintended is worse than refusing to stage it at all.

fn companion_path(header: str) -> str!str

The path a file: section names, rejected when it can't be staged. Mirrors the corpus harness's own checks on these markers, so a fixture that would be staged one way by aipl check and another way by the harness is refused by both instead of being silently skipped by one.
