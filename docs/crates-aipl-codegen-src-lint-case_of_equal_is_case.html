The case_of_equal_is_case lint: shape.case_of() == Circle — reading which case a value is only to compare it with one. That is shape.is_case(Circle), which asks the same question without the detour through a Case.

Exactly one side is a case_of() call. Two of them, a.case_of() == b.case_of(), compare the cases of two values, which is same_case(a, b) and not is_case.

The hit points at the ==, a short line the formatter keeps in place.
